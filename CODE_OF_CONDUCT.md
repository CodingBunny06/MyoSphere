# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

MyoSphere contributors and maintainers are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy and personal boundaries.
- Welcome differing experience levels and perspectives.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, intimidation, and publishing another person's private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. Reports should be made privately to the repository owner through GitHub's private contact mechanisms. Maintainers will review reports and take proportionate action.

This initial policy may be replaced with a recognised community covenant as the project grows.
