# Third-Party Asset and Dataset Policy

MyoSphere may use anatomical models, exercise models, animations, images, fonts, icons, and food datasets. Each asset has its own rights and conditions.

Before adding external material:

1. Record the source and exact version or URL.
2. Verify the licence and whether redistribution and modification are allowed.
3. Record attribution and notice requirements.
4. Check restrictions on commercial use, derivatives, and share-alike obligations.
5. Keep source files and licence notices where required.
6. Do not assume an API's database licence also covers product images or other media.

Prefer original, public-domain, or clearly permissively licensed assets. Do not commit assets with unclear or incompatible terms.
